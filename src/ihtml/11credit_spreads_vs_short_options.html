Bearish: Call comparison

Short ATM call: sell $100,000 call at the $3,800 bid. Bear call credit spread: sell the same call and buy the $103,000 call at the $2,500 ask. Net credit: $1,300. The spread caps loss at $1,700, while the standalone short call has theoretically unlimited loss as BTC rises.

Bullish: Put comparison

Short ATM put: sell $100,000 put at the $3,800 bid. Bull put credit spread: sell the same put and buy the $97,000 put at the $1,800 ask. Net credit: $2,000. The spread caps loss at $1,000. The standalone short put’s worst-case loss is $96,200 at BTC = 0; its loss is bounded because BTC cannot fall below zero.
