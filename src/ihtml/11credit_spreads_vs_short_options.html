At expiration, the short call's loss is theoretically unbounded as BTC rises. A short put's loss is large but bounded because BTC cannot fall below zero. Charts show only the $80,000–$120,000 range. A credit received upfront is not immediate realized profit: the position retains a liability until closed or expired. Real margin requirements and spread recognition depend on the exchange.
