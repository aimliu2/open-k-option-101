Bullish: Call comparison

Long ATM call: buy $100k strike for $4,000. Bull call spread: buy $100k call for $4,000, sell $103k call for $2,300. Net debit: $1,700. The long call has theoretically unlimited upside; the spread caps profit at $1,300.

Bearish: Put comparison

Long ATM put: buy $100k strike for $4,000. Bear put spread: buy $100k put for $4,000, sell $97k put for $1,600. Net debit: $2,400. A long put’s maximum profit is limited because BTC cannot fall below zero. This illustrative bear spread is relatively expensive: maximum profit is only $600 before fees.
