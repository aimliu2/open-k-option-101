Illustrative 30-day expiration P&L, per 1 BTC option exposure. Quotes are taken from btc_itm_atm_otm_long_options_payoff.csv. Buy at ask, sell at bid; excludes trading fees. These are expiration payoffs, not option values before expiry.

Bullish: Call comparison

Long ATM call: buy $100k strike for $4,000.
Bull call spread: buy $100k call for $4,000, sell $103k call for $2,300. Net debit: $1,700.
